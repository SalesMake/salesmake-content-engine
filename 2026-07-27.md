## 4. 2026 cold email deliverability: sending limits that keep you in the inbox
**Source:** Instantly Blog — https://instantly.ai/blog  (fit score 3.0)
**Angle:** Deliverability is the constraint everything else sits on top of; treat sending limits as a floor.

**Linkedin**
```
You can have the best copy in your category and still lose — if it lands in spam.

Deliverability is the constraint everything else sits on top of, and it's the least glamorous thing in outbound, so it's the first thing teams skip.

Per Instantly's benchmarks: safe sending is roughly 50-100 emails per mailbox per day with proper warming, the average reply rate is 3.43%, and top performers clear 10% — largely by keeping emails under 80 words with follow-ups 3-4 days apart, not daily.

Note what those top numbers aren't about: a cleverer subject line. They're about restraint.
```
