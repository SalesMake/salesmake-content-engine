## 1. AI prompts vs cold email templates: what's the difference and when to use each
**Source:** Instantly Blog — https://instantly.ai/blog/ai-prompts-vs-cold-email-templates/  (fit score 4.0)
**Angle:** The prompts-vs-templates debate is a false choice — the real discipline is knowing which job each tool is hired to do at each stage of a sequence.

**Linkedin**
```
The debate between AI prompts and cold email templates is mostly a distraction.

Here's the more useful framing: templates are for predictability, prompts are for relevance. A follow-up asking 'did you get a chance to see my last email?' doesn't need to be personalized — it needs to be consistent and timed well. But a first-touch email landing in a cold inbox needs a reason to exist that's specific to that person. That's where a prompt earns its keep.

The mistake we see most often isn't choosing the wrong tool — it's applying one mode across the entire sequence. First touch treated like a template becomes generic. Follow-ups rebuilt from scratch with AI every time become a time sink with no compounding improvement.

A cleaner workflow: invest the prompt effort at the top of the funnel where differentiation matters, then let templates carry the structural weight further down.

## 2. Why We Are Rebuilding Expandi
**Source:** Expandi Blog — https://expandi.io/blog/we-are-rebuilding-expandi/  (fit score 3.3)
**Angle:** Tool consolidation into 'platforms' is a vendor trend, not a GTM strategy — teams still need to decide what outreach approach the tool is meant to serve before adding more features to it.

**Linkedin**
```
More features inside one tool isn't the same as a clearer GTM motion.

Expandi just announced they're rebuilding into a broader go-to-market platform — more automation, more channels, more done for you. That's a reasonable product bet. But the teams who get the most out of tools like this aren't the ones with the most connected stack. They're the ones who already know what they're trying to do before they open the dashboard.

The consolidation trend in outbound software is real. The risk is that teams treat a new platform as a strategy rather than an enabler of one. If the outreach fundamentals — ICP clarity, message relevance, sequencing logic — aren't solid, a more powerful tool just amplifies the noise faster.
