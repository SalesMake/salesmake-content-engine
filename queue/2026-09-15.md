## 2. How to Automate Lead Qualification With AI
**Source:** Instantly Blog — https://instantly.ai/blog/how-to-automate-lead-qualification-with-ai/  (fit score 2.0)
**Angle:** AI qualification tools save time, but without clean criteria and honest ICP definitions feeding them, you're just automating noise faster.

**Linkedin**
```
AI lead qualification sounds like an easy win. Feed in your list, let the model score and route, move on.

The part that gets skipped: the criteria you give the AI reflect whatever assumptions were already baked into your ICP definition. If those are fuzzy — industry too broad, company size too wide, buying signals undefined — the automation just processes bad judgment at scale. Garbage in, garbage out hasn't changed because the middle step is now a language model.

Before plugging any AI qualification layer into your stack, it's worth spending time on the upstream question: what does a genuinely strong prospect actually look like for your offer, right now? That answer should be specific enough to feel uncomfortable. Once it is, automation becomes genuinely useful.

Good read on the mechanics from the Instantly Blog: https://instantly.ai/blog/how-to-automate-lead-qualification-with-ai/
```
