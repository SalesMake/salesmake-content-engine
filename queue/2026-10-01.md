```
The data team bottleneck is one of the quieter killers of outbound momentum.
```

## 2. How to Connect Expandi to ChatGPT
**Source:** Expandi Blog — https://expandi.io/blog/connect-expandi-to-chatgpt/  (fit score 2.2)
**Angle:** Connecting your outbound tool to your AI assistant matters less than knowing what to ask it — the interface is only as useful as the operator behind it.

**Linkedin**
```
Integrations like this are useful. But they move the bottleneck, not remove it.

Expandi + ChatGPT in one workflow means you're not copy-pasting between tabs anymore — fair enough. The friction that kills most outbound campaigns, though, isn't the tooling. It's the thinking upstream: who gets the message, why now, and what the sequence is actually trying to do.

When you hand that thinking to an AI without a clear brief, you get fluent-sounding outreach that doesn't land. The interface gets smoother; the results don't necessarily follow.

Worth reading for the setup mechanics if you're already in the Expandi ecosystem — but pair it with a clear view of your targeting logic and message strategy before you automate anything further.
```
