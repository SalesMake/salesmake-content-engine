## 2. LinkedIn Outreach Benchmarks for Financial Services (2026)
**Source:** Expandi Blog — https://expandi.io/industry-benchmarks/financial-services/  (fit score 2.2)
**Angle:** Financial services LinkedIn outreach underperforms on connection acceptance but nearly matches the all-industry average once someone has accepted — meaning the real problem is getting in the door, not the conversation after.

**Linkedin**
```
Financial services sits 34th out of 38 industries for LinkedIn connection acceptance — but almost exactly average for replies once someone accepts.

That gap is worth sitting with. It suggests the product or pitch isn't the blocker. The cold approach is. Compliance-heavy sectors have trained buyers to be cautious by default, which means generic outreach gets filtered fast. Personalization has to do more work upfront than in most other verticals.

If you're running LinkedIn outreach into fintech, wealth management, insurance, or banking: the connection note isn't just a formality. It's doing nearly all the heavy lifting. Worth treating it that way.
```
