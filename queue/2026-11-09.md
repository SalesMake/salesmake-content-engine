## 1. AI sales agents: how to onboard new SDRs and AEs faster
**Source:** Instantly Blog — https://instantly.ai/blog/onboard-new-reps-with-ai-sales-agents/  (fit score 2.0)
**Angle:** AI-assisted onboarding is only as good as the process underneath it — structured ramp frameworks matter more than the tools themselves.

**Linkedin**
```
Most ramp problems aren't fixed by better tools — they're fixed by better process.

The Instantly Blog published a week-by-week playbook for using AI sales agents to compress SDR and AE onboarding from months down to four weeks. The structure is worth reading: persona definition in week one, rehearsal drills in week two, live outreach in week three, metric validation in week four. Clean and repeatable.

What it reinforces for us: the reps who ramp fastest aren't the ones handed the best software. They're the ones dropped into a process that removes ambiguity early — who to call, what to say, how to know if it's working. AI can accelerate that loop, but it can't substitute for the loop existing in the first place.

If your onboarding still relies on shadowing and tribal knowledge, the ceiling on your ramp time isn't a training problem. It's a systems problem.
```
