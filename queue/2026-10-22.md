## 3. LinkedIn Outreach Benchmarks for Non-Profit Organizations (2026)
**Source:** Expandi Blog — https://expandi.io/industry-benchmarks/non-profit-organization-management/  (fit score 2.2)
**Angle:** Non-profits get strong connection and note-reply rates but lose momentum after acceptance — the follow-through message is where the sequence actually needs work.

**Linkedin**
```
Non-profit outreach on LinkedIn has a quiet problem hiding inside decent headline numbers.

Per the Expandi Blog's 2026 industry benchmarks, non-profit accounts accept connections at 29.7% and reply to connection notes at 5.0% — both above platform average, ranking 9th and 3rd respectively across 38 industries. Solid. But the post-acceptance message reply rate drops to 8.0%, which is 2.4 points below average and ranks 36th of 38.

That gap tells a specific story: the opening is working, the positioning after the door opens is not. Most sequences treat acceptance as the finish line and send a generic follow-up that doesn't match why the person connected in the first place. The warmth that earned the acceptance evaporates.

If you're running outreach to or within the non-profit sector, the lever isn't the connection request — it's the message that comes right after. That's where sequence design actually matters.
```
