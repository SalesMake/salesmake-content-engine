## 3. How to Run LinkedIn ABM Campaigns That Convert (2026)
**Source:** Expandi Blog — https://expandi.io/blog/abm-campaigns/  (fit score 2.2)
**Angle:** ABM on LinkedIn fails not because of targeting gaps but because the ad layer and the outreach layer are never actually synchronized — and that coordination gap is where deals go quiet.

**Linkedin**
```
ABM campaigns don't fail at the targeting stage. They fail at the handoff.

You can build a tight account list, serve ads to the right titles, and watch engagement metrics tick upward — and still end a quarter with no pipeline. The gap is almost always the same: the people seeing your ads and the people receiving your outreach are operating on separate tracks. No shared timing, no shared message, no one treating the ad impression as a signal to act on.

The Expandi Blog piece on LinkedIn ABM (link below) makes this point clearly: the outreach layer has to be as coordinated as the ad layer. That means knowing when an account is warm enough to contact, what angle to lead with, and who specifically to reach — not just the company, but the people who actually move deals forward.

The practical question isn't 'are we running ABM?' It's 'what happens the moment someone from a target account engages — and who owns that moment?'
```
