## 1. How Much Does Lead Generation Cost?
**Source:** Expandi Blog — https://expandi.io/blog/how-much-does-lead-generation-cost/  (fit score 4.4)
**Angle:** The real lead-gen cost question isn't the price tag per channel — it's whether you're comparing like-for-like outputs, because a cheap CPL from paid ads and a cheap CPL from outbound rarely buy you the same quality of conversation.

**Linkedin**
```
Most "how much does lead generation cost?" breakdowns stop at the invoice. That's the wrong place to stop.

The meaningful comparison isn't agency retainer vs. SDR salary vs. ad spend vs. automation tool. It's: what does each model actually produce at the end of the funnel? A low cost-per-lead from a broad paid channel and a higher cost-per-lead from tightly targeted outbound can swap positions entirely once you factor in close rate, sales cycle length, and how much hand-holding each lead needs.

Before you benchmark your spend, it's worth asking: are you pricing inputs or pricing outcomes?
```

```
→ Agency retainer: predictable spend, execution offloaded
```
