## 1. 5 Cold Email Sequences That Get Replies in 2026
**Source:** Expandi Blog — https://expandi.io/blog/email-sequence-outreach/  (fit score 4.4)
**Angle:** The real challenge with cold email sequences isn't the templates — it's building a sequence logic that respects compliance constraints while still creating enough touchpoints to earn a reply.

**Linkedin**
```
Cold email sequences get a lot of coverage, but most of the advice focuses on copy tweaks. The harder problem — especially in 2025/2026 — is designing a sequence that holds up under GDPR and privacy scrutiny without becoming so cautious it loses all momentum.

A few things that actually matter more than subject line formulas:
— The gap between touches: too fast feels like spam, too slow loses context
— Your reason for each follow-up (not just 'just checking in')
— Whether your list was sourced in a way you can actually defend

Sequences aren't just a copy problem. They're a process and compliance problem wearing a copy costume.

## 3. AI Lead Generation: How to Apply AI to Email Outreach
**Source:** Hunter.io Blog — https://hunter.io/blog/ai-lead-generation-how-to-apply-ai-to-email-outreach/  (fit score 2.0)
**Angle:** AI in outbound works best as a capability multiplier for a skilled operator, not a replacement — the human judgment layer is still what separates good outreach from noise.

**Linkedin**
```
AI won't fix a weak outreach strategy. It will just execute it faster.

There's a useful framing in a recent Hunter.io Blog post on AI lead generation: the effective setup is a skilled human operator assisted by AI — not replaced by it. The piece focuses on using outreach platforms via MCP, but the underlying principle matters more than the tooling: AI amplifies whatever judgment you bring to it.

In practice, that means the inputs still have to be right. Who you're targeting, why now, what you're actually saying — AI doesn't resolve those questions. It just removes the friction once you've answered them. The teams getting value from AI in outbound are the ones who were already thinking clearly about those fundamentals.
