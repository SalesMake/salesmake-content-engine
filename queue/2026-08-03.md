## 2. Outbound vs inbound sales: key differences, pros, and when to use each
**Source:** Instantly Blog — https://instantly.ai/blog/outbound-vs-inbound-sales/  (fit score 5.0)
**Angle:** The real unlock isn't choosing outbound or inbound — it's using inbound intent signals as the trigger for outbound sequences, which most teams still treat as separate machines.

**Linkedin**
```
Most outbound vs. inbound debates miss the point.

The question isn't which motion is better — it's how quickly your team spots an intent signal from inbound and turns it into a targeted outbound touch. A prospect downloads a guide, visits a pricing page, or engages with a webinar. That's a window. Most teams let it close while the lead sits in a nurture queue.

The Instantly Blog has a solid breakdown of both motions — benchmarks, CAC comparisons, when to lean into each — worth a read if you're structuring pipeline strategy: https://instantly.ai/blog/outbound-vs-inbound-sales/

The bit that stands out: per their analysis, B2B teams running both motions together outperform single-motion teams on revenue growth. That tracks with what we see too. The gap usually isn't strategy — it's execution. Inbound and outbound live in separate tools, separate teams, separate cadences. Nobody owns the handoff.
```
