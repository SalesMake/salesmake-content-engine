## 3. Lead Follow Up on LinkedIn: When to Message, What to Say, and When to Stop
**Source:** Expandi Blog — https://expandi.io/blog/linkedin-follow-up-message/  (fit score 4.4)
**Angle:** More follow-up messages hurt reply rates — sequences should earn each touchpoint, not just add more of them.

**Linkedin**
```
Most outbound sequences are too long — and this data backs that up.

Per the Expandi Blog's analysis of 2,966 LinkedIn campaigns, 3-message sequences outperformed longer ones. Campaigns with 5+ messages actually did worse than sending just one. The takeaway isn't 'send less.' It's 'send with purpose.' Every follow-up should have a reason to exist beyond 'just checking in.'

In practice, that means your second and third messages need to add something: a different angle, a relevant proof point, a question that shifts the frame. If you can't articulate why message four deserves to land in someone's inbox, it probably shouldn't.

Volume isn't the lever. Relevance is.

What's your current sequence length — and do each of those messages have a job to do?
```
