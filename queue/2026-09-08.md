**Angle:** Staffing leads all 38 industries on LinkedIn outreach response rates — which tells you the audience is reachable, but also that standing out in a crowded, high-volume category still requires message discipline.

**Linkedin**
```
Staffing & recruiting ranks #1 across all 38 industries tracked for LinkedIn outreach — highest connection acceptance, highest reply-to-note rate, highest post-acceptance reply rate. Per the Expandi Blog benchmarks, the gap over the platform average is meaningful on every metric.

The obvious read: recruiters have trained the LinkedIn audience to accept and respond. The less obvious read: that same familiarity is a double-edged sword. When a category dominates volume, recipients get selective fast. Above-average rates don't protect you from below-average messages.
```
