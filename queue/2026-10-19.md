## 1. Sales Lead Lists 101: How to Build a Prospecting List That Actually Gets Replies in 2026
**Source:** Expandi Blog — https://expandi.io/blog/how-to-build-a-prospect-list/  (fit score 5.5)
**Angle:** Bad reply rates are usually a list problem, not a copy problem — the prospect file deserves the same scrutiny as the message.

**Linkedin**
```
When a cold campaign underperforms, the instinct is to rewrite the opening line. Usually that's the wrong fix.

The Expandi Blog has a solid primer on building prospect lists that's worth a read: https://expandi.io/blog/how-to-build-a-prospect-list/. The point that stands out most to us: a lead list isn't just a column of names and emails — it should record *why* each person belongs there and how closely they match your ICP. Without that, you're essentially A/B testing copy on the wrong audience.

In practice, we see this constantly. A sequence gets blamed for low reply rates, the team iterates on subject lines for two weeks, and the underlying issue is that half the list never fit the ICP to begin with. Tightening the list — even by 30% — often moves reply rates more than any messaging tweak.

Before your next campaign refresh, it's worth asking: when did you last audit the list itself, not just the copy?
```
