## 3. How HubSpot Runs CRM Enrichment at Scale with Clay - The GTM with Clay Blog
**Source:** Clay Blog — https://www.clay.com/blog/top-1-how-hubspot-runs-clay-across-a-60-million-contact-crm  (fit score 3.6)
**Angle:** Running enrichment at HubSpot's scale surfaces a truth most teams ignore: data quality decisions compound — and the ROI math only works if you're ruthless about what you actually enrich.

**Linkedin**
```
Most teams treat CRM enrichment as a one-time project. HubSpot treats it as ongoing infrastructure.

The Clay Blog published a breakdown of how HubSpot's Faris Sumadi runs enrichment across a CRM with 30M+ companies. The lessons are interesting — but the one that stands out for outbound practitioners is the discipline around *what not to enrich*. At that scale, enriching everything is wasteful. Knowing which records actually matter to your pipeline is the real leverage point.

For most companies, the problem isn't access to enrichment tools. It's that nobody has drawn a clear line between the records worth spending on and the ones that inflate your numbers without ever moving a deal. That upstream decision shapes everything downstream: sequencing, personalization, routing, reporting.

If your team is sitting on a large CRM and enrichment feels expensive or messy, it's worth asking whether the data strategy came before the tooling — or after.
```
