**Source:** Clay Blog — https://www.clay.com/blog/ai-email-personalization-examples  (fit score 4.8)
**Angle:** 23 examples is useful, but the real question is whether personalization is doing signal work or just decoration — and most teams can't tell the difference.

**Linkedin**
```
Most AI personalization isn't personalization. It's decoration.

Referencing someone's LinkedIn bio or recent post isn't a signal — it's a pattern. Prospects have seen it. The icebreaker that name-drops their company podcast or congratulates them on a funding round reads as automated the moment it lands, because everyone else is running the same Clay workflow with the same ChatGPT prompt.

The Clay Blog just published 23 AI email personalization examples worth scanning (link below). It's a genuinely useful reference. But what it can't tell you is whether your personalization is tied to a real reason to reach out — or just bolted on to make a template feel warmer. That distinction is where open rates stop mattering and replies start.

Before scaling a sequence, it's worth asking: does the personalized detail *justify* the outreach, or does it just acknowledge the recipient exists? The former earns a reply. The latter earns an unsubscribe.
```
