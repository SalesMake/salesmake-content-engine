**Angle:** Self-serve analytics agents reduce the data-team bottleneck that slows GTM decisions — but the real lesson for outbound teams is that waiting on internal queues is a solvable problem, not a fixed constraint.

**Linkedin**
```
Most GTM teams don't have a data problem. They have a queue problem.
```
