## 2. LinkedIn InMail vs Message: Differences & When to Use Each
**Source:** Expandi Blog — https://expandi.io/blog/linkedin-inmail-vs-message/  (fit score 4.4)
**Angle:** The InMail vs message debate isn't about access — it's about knowing which signal (connection vs cold reach) fits where your prospect actually sits in your funnel.

**Linkedin**
```
Most LinkedIn outreach decisions come down to budget, not strategy. That's backwards.

The real question isn't 'can I afford InMail credits?' — it's 'where does this prospect sit relative to me, and what signal does my message need to carry?' A connection request with a short note says something different from an InMail. A follow-up to a 1st-degree connection says something different again. Conflating them because one is free and one isn't is how you end up with volume that goes nowhere.

Per the Expandi Blog's H1 2026 data, InMail to Open Profiles averaged an 8.37% reply rate — which sounds reasonable until you ask what the connection-then-message sequence looked like for the same audience. The channel choice and the sequence design are inseparable, and the latter is where most teams underinvest.

Are you picking your LinkedIn outreach method based on what you can send, or based on what actually fits the conversation you're trying to start?
```
