## 1. 7 Ethical LinkedIn outreach best practices to follow in 2026
**Source:** Expandi Blog — https://expandi.io/blog/ethical-linkedin-outreach-best-practices/  (fit score 3.3)
**Angle:** The shift from 'avoid a ban' to 'avoid breaking trust' is the real LinkedIn maturity arc — and most outreach still hasn't made that leap.

**Linkedin**
```
LinkedIn outreach has a maturity problem.

Most teams got past the 'don't get banned' phase years ago. But a lot of sequences are still stuck there mentally — optimised for delivery, not for the person receiving them. The question worth asking in 2025 isn't 'will this get flagged?' It's 'would someone who received this feel respected or processed?'

That distinction changes a lot: how much personalisation actually earns a reply vs. performs it, when to stop following up, and whether your connection request is a genuine opener or just a funnel entry point dressed up in friendly language.

Ethical outreach and effective outreach aren't in tension. They converge when the goal is a real conversation rather than a volume metric.

Expandi Blog has a practical breakdown of where the lines sit in 2026: https://expandi.io/blog/ethical-linkedin-outreach-best-practices/
```
