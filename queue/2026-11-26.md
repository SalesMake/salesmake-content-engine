## 2. How to validate AI-generated prospect data before it hits your CRM
**Source:** Instantly Blog — https://instantly.ai/blog/validate-ai-generated-prospect-data/  (fit score 3.0)
**Angle:** AI enrichment failures are a list-hygiene problem first — validation discipline is what separates usable pipeline from expensive noise in your CRM.

**Linkedin**
```
AI-enriched prospect data can look clean and still be wrong.

The problem isn't the enrichment step — it's what happens (or doesn't happen) before the data lands in your CRM. Hallucinated job titles, stale emails, mismatched company sizes: none of these announce themselves. They just quietly inflate your bounce rate and skew every attribution call you make downstream.

The Instantly Blog put together a six-step validation framework worth bookmarking — waterfall verification, field-level checks, the works. The underlying principle is straightforward: treat AI output as a first draft, not a finished record.

One thing the article doesn't emphasize enough for outbound specifically: validation logic needs to match your sending cadence. A list you're warming over 8 weeks has different tolerance thresholds than one going into an aggressive short-burst sequence. The check that keeps bounces below 2% isn't universal — it depends on volume, domain age, and how many unverified sources fed the enrichment.

Worth a read if data quality is quietly costing you deliverability: https://instantly.ai/blog/validate-ai-generated-prospect-data/
```
