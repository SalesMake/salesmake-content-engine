## 1. Mass Messaging & Automation on LinkedIn (2026 Guide)
**Source:** Expandi Blog — https://expandi.io/blog/mass-message-on-linkedin/  (fit score 3.3)
**Angle:** Automation compresses the time cost of LinkedIn outreach, but the real leverage comes from what you do with that saved time — better targeting and message logic, not just higher send volume.

**Linkedin**
```
Automation on LinkedIn is mostly a time argument, not a quality argument.

Per the Expandi Blog, manually working through 1,000 prospects — profile review, personalisation, send — can eat roughly 83 hours. A tool collapses that. But the hours you recover only matter if the underlying message logic is sound. Most LinkedIn outreach at volume fails because the sequencing and targeting decisions stay the same while the send rate goes up.

The better use of saved time: tighten your ICP, write fewer template variants that actually match different buying situations, and slow down the follow-up cadence. Automation handles repetition. It doesn't fix a weak offer or a misread audience.
```
