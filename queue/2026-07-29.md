**Angle:** Automating inbound follow-up is worthwhile, but the sequencing logic matters more than the tooling — most teams get the order of enrichment, scoring, and outreach backwards.

**Linkedin**
```
Automating inbound lead outreach sounds straightforward until you actually build it.

The order of operations is where most setups quietly fail. Teams enrich after they score, score before they know intent, or fire a sequence before the lead even matches an ICP. The result is a technically 'automated' workflow that still produces noise — just faster noise.

The more useful framing: enrichment gates scoring, scoring gates sequence enrollment, and every step should have an exit condition. If a lead doesn't clear the scoring threshold, the automation should know what to do with them — not just drop them.
```
