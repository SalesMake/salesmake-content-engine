```
Apollo is a prospecting database with sequencing bolted on. **Source:** Expandi Blog — https://expandi.io/blog/b2b-prospecting-tool/  (fit score 2.2)
```
**Angle:** A bloated prospecting stack isn't a tool problem — it's an integration problem, and adding more tools rarely solves it.

**Linkedin**
```
More tools, same struggle.

The Expandi Blog just published a roundup of 20 B2B prospecting tools for 2026, and it's a useful read — but the real diagnosis is buried in the intro: most teams already have a CRM, a data provider, and an outreach tool. The problem isn't the tools. It's that they don't talk to each other, so reps are still doing manual list work, chasing stale data, and reaching out at the wrong moment.

Adding tool number 21 won't fix that. What actually helps is stepping back and asking: where does the handoff break? Is it between data enrichment and sequencing? Between CRM signals and trigger-based outreach? Once you know where the gap is, the solution is usually simpler — and cheaper — than another platform.

If you're evaluating your outbound stack and not sure where the friction really lives, it's worth mapping the workflow before you open another free trial.
```
