**Angle:** Channel-switching without context isn't multi-channel outreach — it's just noise, and the fix is a single prospect record that governs both LinkedIn and email sequencing.

**Linkedin**
```
A prospect replies to your email on Tuesday. Your LinkedIn message lands Thursday — with zero acknowledgment that the conversation already started. That's not a follow-up. That's a reset.

The mechanics matter less than the mindset shift. Multi-channel outreach isn't about adding channels. It's about making sure every channel knows what the others have already done.
```
