**Linkedin**
```
Most teams pick a data enrichment tool based on the headline price. They find out the real cost later — usually when the bill arrives.

Per-record, subscription, and credit-based models all have legitimate use cases, but each one has a different failure mode. Credit models punish you for failed lookups.
```
