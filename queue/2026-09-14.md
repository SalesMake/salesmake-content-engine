## 1. AI sales agent vs AI SDR: what's the difference? (2026 category guide for sales leaders)
**Source:** Instantly Blog — https://instantly.ai/blog/ai-sales-agent-vs-ai-sdr/  (fit score 4.0)
**Angle:** The SDR vs. agent distinction matters less than understanding what autonomy level your pipeline can actually absorb right now.

**Linkedin**
```
The label 'AI SDR' and 'AI sales agent' are being used almost interchangeably right now — but the difference has real buying implications.

The short version: an AI SDR automates discrete tasks (sequencing, personalization, follow-up). An AI agent orchestrates the whole workflow — deciding what happens next, not just executing a step you defined. That scope of autonomy changes how you think about deliverability controls, CRM hygiene, and where a human needs to stay in the loop.

Before evaluating any of these tools, the more useful question is: what level of autonomy does your current pipeline setup actually support? Most teams buy the agent and run it like an SDR tool — which wastes the capability and sometimes creates blind spots in reporting.

## 2. AI sales agents for B2B company research: the complete guide for outbound teams
**Source:** Instantly Blog — https://instantly.ai/blog/ai-sales-agents-b2b-company-research/  (fit score 3.0)
**Angle:** AI research agents are most valuable not for the data they find, but for the decision they eliminate — whether to spend an hour on a prospect before you even know if they're worth a call.

**Linkedin**
```
The real win with AI research agents isn't speed. It's removing the judgment call nobody talks about.

Before any outbound rep writes a first touch, there's an invisible tax: deciding whether a prospect is even worth researching. That 30-second gut check, repeated across a list of 200 accounts, becomes hours of quiet friction that never shows up in a pipeline report.

AI agents that automate firmographic and intent data gathering don't just compress research time — they lower the threshold for what gets researched at all. More accounts get a proper look. Fewer slip through because someone didn't have bandwidth on a Tuesday afternoon.
