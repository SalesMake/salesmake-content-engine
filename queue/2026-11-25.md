## 2. LinkedIn Employee Advocacy That Books Meetings: Turn Your Team’s Posts Into Pipeline
**Source:** Expandi Blog — https://expandi.io/blog/linkedin-employee-advocacy/  (fit score 2.2)
**Angle:** Impressions without intent signals is just noise — employee advocacy only earns pipeline when someone follows the trail from post to prospect.

**Linkedin**
```
40,000 impressions and zero new meetings. That's the quiet failure state of most employee advocacy programs.

The Expandi Blog recently put a name to the gap: teams celebrate getting posts out the door and then treat the resulting likes and profile visits as vanity metrics rather than buying signals. But someone who reads a rep's post and clicks through to their profile is not a passive scroller — they're a warm unknown. The question is whether your process is set up to notice them.

Employee advocacy earns its keep when there's a clear handoff between content activity and outbound motion: who monitors profile views after a post goes live, what the follow-up message looks like, how quickly it goes out. Without that, you're essentially running a brand awareness exercise and calling it pipeline.
```
