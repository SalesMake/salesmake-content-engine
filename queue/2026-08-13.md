## 3. 20 Cold Email Call to Action Examples That Work in 2026
**Source:** Expandi Blog — https://expandi.io/blog/cold-email-call-to-action/  (fit score 2.2)
**Angle:** The CTA isn't the closing line — it's a calibration of how much trust already exists, and most cold emails ignore that entirely.

**Linkedin**
```
Most cold email advice obsesses over subject lines and openers. The CTA at the end gets treated as a formality — 'Let me know if you're interested' or 'Worth a quick call?'

But the ending is where the ask either fits the relationship or doesn't. A prospect who's never heard of you doesn't owe you 30 minutes. A low-friction CTA — a yes/no question, a specific resource, a single-click reply prompt — respects that gap. A meeting request doesn't.

Expandi's blog put together 20 examples pulled from campaigns that actually generated replies, and the pattern that holds across all of them is straightforward: the ask scales with familiarity. Cold contact gets a small ask. Warmer sequences earn a bigger one.
```
