## 2. The Automated Growth Plays Behind Clay’s Self-Serve and Enterprise Motions - The GTM with Clay Blog
**Source:** Clay Blog — https://www.clay.com/blog/how-clay-uses-clay-automated-growth-plays  (fit score 2.4)
**Angle:** Clay's own growth playbook reveals a principle most outbound teams underuse: automation works best when it's triggered by account behavior, not just time-based sequences.

**Linkedin**
```
Most outbound automation is calendar-driven. Clay's internal growth team does something different — their plays fire based on what an account actually does (or stops doing).

The Clay Blog breaks down the always-on sequences their team runs: trial conversion, account rescue, and enterprise prospecting. What's worth noting isn't the tooling — it's the logic underneath. Each play is anchored to a signal. An account goes quiet? A different message goes out. A target enterprise account shows a hiring spike? A door opens. The trigger defines the relevance, and relevance is what gets replies.

Most teams build sequences first and bolt on personalization later. Flipping that — starting from the signal and building the message around it — tends to produce cleaner results and less noise for prospects.
```
