## 3. Email Drip Campaigns: A B2B Guide to Drips Across LinkedIn and Email (+ Examples)
**Source:** Expandi Blog — https://expandi.io/blog/email-drip-campaign/  (fit score 2.2)
**Angle:** The real lift in drip campaigns comes from trigger-based sequencing across channels, not adding more touchpoints on a fixed timer.

**Linkedin**
```
Most B2B drip campaigns fail not because they're too short, but because they ignore timing and treat every lead the same.

A drip that fires five emails on a seven-day clock regardless of what the prospect did is just scheduled noise. The ones that actually earn replies are built around signals — a profile view, a clicked link, a form fill — and they use whatever channel makes sense at that moment, not just email by default.

Expandi Blog recently published a solid practical guide on this: pairing LinkedIn steps with email sequences rather than running them in silos. Per their H1 2026 data, the majority of campaigns on their platform already combine both channels. That tracks with what we see in outbound generally — channel sequencing matters, but only if the triggers and the message logic are doing real work underneath.

The question worth asking before you build another sequence: what does a lead have to *do* for the next message to make sense? If the answer is 'nothing, it just sends on day X,' the sequence probably needs a rethink.
```
