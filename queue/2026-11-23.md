## 2. Use Clay and Claude to build your GTM growth engine - The GTM with Clay Blog
**Source:** Clay Blog — https://www.clay.com/blog/clay-and-claude-gtm-growth-engine  (fit score 3.6)
**Angle:** Clay + Claude workflows lower the execution barrier for weekly prospecting, but tooling alone doesn't replace the strategic decisions about who to target and what to say.

**Linkedin**
```
Automated prospecting workflows are only as good as the thinking behind them.

Clay's blog walks through pairing Clay with Claude for Small Business to build a weekly prospecting loop — list building, enrichment, AI-assisted messaging, all on a recurring schedule. It's a solid technical walkthrough for teams ready to get hands-on.

What it doesn't cover (and what usually determines whether these setups actually produce pipeline): the upstream decisions. Who are you targeting and why? What signal are you using to prioritize? What does your message need to do in the first two sentences? A well-built workflow running against a vague ICP or a generic message template will just automate mediocrity at higher volume.

The tools are more accessible than ever. The judgment about how to use them is still the hard part.

Worth a read if you're evaluating this stack: https://www.clay.com/blog/clay-and-claude-gtm-growth-engine
```
