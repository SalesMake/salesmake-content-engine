## 2. AI for Sales Prospecting: The LinkedIn Playbook for 2026
**Source:** Expandi Blog — https://expandi.io/blog/ai-for-sales-prospecting/  (fit score 2.2)
**Angle:** AI amplifies whatever effort level you bring to prospecting — so low-effort outreach scaled by AI just produces low-effort noise faster, while genuinely researched, targeted outreach becomes scalable for the first time.

**Linkedin**
```
AI doesn't flatten the difference between lazy and thoughtful prospecting — it widens it.

There's a useful piece on the Expandi Blog about AI for LinkedIn prospecting (https://expandi.io/blog/ai-for-sales-prospecting/) that makes this point clearly: AI lowers the floor as well as raises the ceiling. Point it at volume with no real targeting logic and you get faster spam. Use it to scale work that was already precise — solid ICP definition, a genuine reason to reach out, a relevant message — and suddenly you can do that at a pace that wasn't previously possible.

The trap most teams fall into is treating AI as a replacement for thinking rather than a multiplier of it. The research step, the decision about who actually matters to contact, the question of what they'd actually care about — none of that goes away. AI just means you don't have to choose between doing it well and doing it at scale anymore.

Are you using AI to go faster, or to go further with the same rigour?
```
