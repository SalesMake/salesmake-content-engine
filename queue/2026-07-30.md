## 3. Introducing Account Research Agents to Clay  - The GTM with Clay Blog
**Source:** Clay Blog — https://www.clay.com/blog/account-research-agents  (fit score 2.4)
**Angle:** AI account research agents shift the bottleneck in outbound from data gathering to decision-making — which changes how you should think about sequencing expansion and re-engagement plays.

**Linkedin**
```
Account research has always been the slow, leaky part of outbound. You spend cycles pulling intent signals, firmographic changes, and product usage hints — then by the time a rep acts on them, the window has moved.

Clay just released Account Research Agents (covered on the Clay Blog), which automate that continuous monitoring so expansion and re-engagement plays can trigger on fresh intelligence rather than stale list pulls. The interesting GTM implication isn't just speed — it's that the bottleneck shifts. When research is always-on, the constraint becomes deciding *which accounts deserve a play right now* and *what that play should actually say*. Sequence logic and messaging judgment matter more, not less.

If you're building out expansion motions or trying to time re-engagement better, it's worth thinking about where humans stay in the loop. Automation surfaces the signal; someone still has to decide what it means. Are your reps set up to act on real-time account changes — or are they still working from last quarter's data?
```
